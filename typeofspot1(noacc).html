MASBATE CITY

BUNTOD REEF MARINE SANCTUARY AND SANDBAR

A 251-hectare Marine Protected Area (MPA) located off the waters of Masbate City, it is best known for its abundance of helmet shells,spowdery white 1-hectare sandbar as well as a healthy numberof mangroves at the far end of the sandbar. The sanctuary serves as a barrier island protecting the main land from wave attack, storm surge, tidal range,and sediment transport. Part of island hopping package, swimming, snorkeling, scuba diving, sunset watching, beach combing, and water sport activities
